I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Brandeis University, advised by [Professor Pengyu Hong](https://www.cs.brandeis.edu/~hong/). My research interests are in generative AI for real-world applications — building models that are efficient, reliable, and interpretable. At the moment, my work mainly focuses on:

- **Controllable multimodal generation**: developing methods to steer diffusion and multimodal models with structured text, visual, or structural conditions, together with robust fusion strategies across modalities.

- **Text-to-image diffusion for stable, high-quality synthesis**: improving generation quality and consistency through better inference (sampling and guidance) and lightweight model adaptation (fine-tuning or knowledge editing of pretrained backbones).



Before Brandeis, I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Statistics from the **University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign (UIUC)**. I then completed my M.S. in Data Science at **Columbia University**.

Outside research, I enjoy working out, cooking, making cocktails, and traveling. If you share similar research interests, I’d be very happy to connect and chat more—feel free to reach out 😊
