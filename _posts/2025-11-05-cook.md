Simple homemade **Eggs Benedict**:

- Toasted base (bread / muffin)
- Poached egg
- A slice of ham or smoked salmon
- Hollandaise sauce on top

---

## Hollandaise, very roughly

A basic hollandaise is just **egg yolk + butter + acid**:

1. Whisk egg yolks with a bit of lemon juice (and a splash of water) in a heatproof bowl.  
2. Set the bowl over gentle steam (not boiling water), keep whisking until it slightly thickens.  
3. Slowly drizzle in melted butter while whisking constantly, until it turns smooth and creamy.  
4. Season with salt, pepper, and a bit more lemon juice if needed.

Then just pour it over the poached egg and eat before it cools down.
