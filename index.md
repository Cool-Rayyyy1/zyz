## About Me

I am now a **fourth-year Ph.D. student** in Computer Science at Brandeis University, advised by [Professor Pengyu Hong](https://www.cs.brandeis.edu/~hong/). Before joining Brandeis, I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Statistics from the **University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign (UIUC)** and my M.S. in Data Science from **Columbia University**.

My research focuses on **efficient image generation with diffusion and flow-based models**. I study training-time distillation and inference acceleration, as well as how inference-time choices, such as seed selection, affect generation quality and alignment with text prompts.

I am also interested in **video generation and synthesis**, particularly maintaining long-term temporal consistency and coherence across frames. If you share similar interests or have ideas to discuss, please feel free to reach out—I would love to exchange ideas and explore potential collaborations.
